❓ FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does task posting work? +
Simply create an account, click "Post a Task", fill in the details, and submit. Helpers in your area will see your task.
Is my payment secure? +
Yes! We use secure payment gateways and hold funds in escrow until you confirm completion.
How are helpers vetted? +
Helpers go through identity verification, skill assessments, and background checks.
Can I cancel a task? +
Yes, tasks can be cancelled before a helper is assigned. Cancellation fees may apply.
